# Submission

Live app: https://newsletter-assistant-navy.vercel.app

GitHub: https://github.com/tejasmmali/Newsletter-Assistant

This is a research and writing workspace for *This Week in AI*. An editor adds public articles, gets a brief whose claims are checked against quotes in the extract, and revises a draft with deterministic flags plus optional rewrites that cannot drop numbers, names, hedges, or jargon. The sample packet is in `docs/sample/`. The author still has to edit the newsletter in their own voice before sending it.

## Pre-send checklist

- [ ] Edit `docs/sample/final-newsletter.md` in your own voice.
- [ ] Replace the `TODO (author must fill in honestly)` block in `README.md` with what you personally implemented, tested, and verified.
- [ ] Rotate the Gemini API key, then update the Vercel env value. Do not paste the new key into the repo or into chat.
- [ ] Click the bookmarklet on a real article with the real `INGEST_TOKEN` and confirm the source appears.
- [ ] Attach your resume to the email.
- [ ] Email hardeep@thisweekinai.club yourself. This packet does not send that email.
